How Stillvaro 2.1 looks after your local practice.

What stays on the device

Practice answers, mistakes, guidance, timings, review dates, test results, milestone achievements and dates, saved positions, and preferences are processed and stored locally. The learning scheduler runs on the device. Stillvaro has no account server, app-initiated practice uploads, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics SDK in the reviewed source.

Support and privacy links open your browser. Sending an email is your choice; the app does not attach practice history automatically. Apple services, website hosting, email, and device backups have their own data handling.

How a save is protected

Stillvaro stores versioned practice files in its app container and uses the operating system’s storage protections. It writes complete saves atomically, validates data when reading it, and keeps the previous valid save as a local recovery copy. Preferences use app-only storage.

The app does not add its own password-encrypted file format, account password, or remote recovery service. The recovery copy lives on the same device as the main save. It can help with a damaged or missing save, but it is not a separate backup if the device is lost.

If saved practice cannot be read

When a valid previous save is available, Stillvaro can recover it and tells you that it has done so. It preserves damaged or unsupported data instead of silently treating it as fresh progress. A file written by a newer app version can require an update. Follow the recovery message before erasing anything.

Backups and moving devices

Stillvaro does not automatically synchronize progress between devices or import progress files. Device backups may include Stillvaro data depending on your settings. Manage backup availability and restoration through your device’s backup service. Stillvaro cannot promise that an external backup is available or current.

Milestone records

Milestone achievements and their earned dates are part of the local practice save. Turning off Milestone badges changes their presentation; it does not delete achievements or test results. Erasing progress removes these records with the rest of the local save.

Erasing local data

Choose Settings → Erase all progress and settings → Erase All Data. After confirmation, the app removes local practice records, recovery and damaged-save files, and preferences, then starts fresh. If file removal fails, it reports the failure. Erasure cannot be undone inside the app.

Local erasure does not remove device backups, files outside the app, or messages already sent to support. Those copies need to be managed separately.

Report a problem

Email support@mholson.com with the app version, device and OS version, and a description of what happened. For a suspected security issue, describe the steps privately by email and include only the information needed to understand it. Do not include device passwords or unrelated personal data.

See the privacy policy for the full data-handling description.
