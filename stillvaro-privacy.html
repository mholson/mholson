Stillvaro privacy policy

How Stillvaro handles local records, backups, external links, and support.

Updated November 1, 2026. This policy describes Stillvaro 2.1.

Stillvaro is developed by Mark Olson. It is designed for private practice on your iPhone or iPad and does not require an account.

Practice stays on your device

Stillvaro’s app code does not automatically upload your practice data to the developer or a third-party service. It has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

The app stores the information it needs to continue your practice, including:

  • Your place in π, entered digits, completed groups, and mistakes.
  • Guided practice, reference exposure, recall attempts, timing, review dates, and connections between groups.
  • Memory-test results, personal bests, and milestone achievements with their earned dates.
  • Anchor notes retained from earlier versions, where present. The latest interface no longer offers cue editing.
  • Appearance, haptics, playback, help, and other preferences.

These records are used on your device to present your progress and milestones, resume sessions, and choose practice and reviews. Turning off Milestone badges hides their presentation while retaining achievements and test results. The learning scheduler runs locally. It does not send your answers to an AI service.

Saving and device backups

Practice saves automatically in the app’s local storage. The app keeps a local recovery copy and may retain damaged files so they are not silently discarded. If saved progress cannot be read, the app explains the problem and any available next step. When possible, it recovers the previous valid save automatically and tells you.

Stillvaro does not synchronize progress between devices or provide a progress-file importer.

iOS device backups may include app data depending on your device and backup settings. Local learning records use the device’s storage protections.

Apple services and external links

Apple may provide the developer with App Store reports, including usage and diagnostic information shared according to your Apple settings. Stillvaro does not add an analytics SDK to do this. App usage reporting through Apple is subject to your choice to share analytics and to Apple’s privacy measures. See Apple’s analytics information and Apple’s privacy policy.

Opening Support or Privacy policy leaves the app for a website. Website hosting, browsing, and email services can process information needed to provide those services. This policy describes the Stillvaro app; external services have their own privacy practices.

Contacting support

If you email support@mholson.com, you choose to provide your email address, message, and any attachments. This information can be used to respond to your request. The app does not attach learning records to support messages automatically. Include only information you choose to share. You can contact the same address about a support message or to request its deletion.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you erase it or remove the app and its data. In Settings → Erase all progress and settings, confirm Erase All Data to remove saved learning records, local recovery files, and preferences. This starts a fresh practice state. The developer cannot remotely inspect or erase your local app storage.

Removing the app from your device also removes its app data under iOS’s deletion behavior; offloading keeps data. Device backups and any files stored outside the app are separate. Delete or manage those copies using the relevant service. Changing the app’s local data does not delete a support email already sent.

Children and changes

The app does not ask for age, identity, or account details and does not send a child’s practice history to the developer. Any future change to these data practices will be reflected in this policy with an updated date.

For privacy questions, contact Mark Olson at support@mholson.com.
