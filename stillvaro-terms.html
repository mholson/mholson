Prepared update for Stillvaro 2.1: October 30, 2026

Stillvaro is a personal learning app by Mark Olson for exploring and practising the digits of π.

App license

For downloads from the App Store, the applicable App Store license agreement governs your use of the app. Unless a custom agreement is supplied on its product page, this is Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. This page explains Stillvaro’s features and use; it does not replace that agreement or limit mandatory consumer rights.

Practice and progress

Stillvaro provides visual cues, practice, and review scheduling. No claim is made that using Stillvaro will help you memorize the digits of π. Results depend on how you use it. Its progress displays describe activity in the app; they are not guarantees of learning, permanent retention, or improvements in general cognitive ability. Stillvaro is not a medical or diagnostic service.

The 2.1 app described here needs no account and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Any price shown by the App Store applies to the download. Apple handles App Store transactions and refund requests under its terms.

Your local library

Your progress saves automatically on your device, with local recovery handling. Stillvaro does not automatically synchronize progress between devices or import progress files. Device backups are managed through your device’s settings.

Erase all progress and settings removes the app’s local learning records, recovery files, and preferences after confirmation. It does not remove separate device backups or files stored outside the app. Storage formats and features can change between releases.

Privacy and support

Read the Stillvaro privacy policy for information about local records, Apple services, backups, and support messages.

Contact support@mholson.com for help or questions. Include your app and iOS versions so the issue can be understood.
