Your iPad workspace

iPhone uses portrait orientation. On iPad, Stillvaro uses one resizable window and follows system rotation. Wide windows place the sequence beside the dial; narrow windows stack them. Choose Practice, Explore π, Spiral, or Your memory in the top navigation; tap the current view’s name to switch in narrow windows. Focus on practice reduces the history. Short windows move Memory, Guide, and Watch into Practice options. Scroll when more room is needed. Your input and place stay saved while you resize.

On iPad, choose Settings → iPad → Dial position → Left or Right to place the dial on either side of your sequence in wide landscape windows. The choice is saved. Portrait, narrow windows, and centered Focus mode keep their existing layout.

Four digits. One shape. Start with 3141, including π’s leading 3. Leave out the decimal point. Your place saves automatically; there is no countdown.

Your practice rhythm

StepWhat to do
1 · GuideFollow the highlighted digits and watch the shape form.
2 · MemoryRecall the group yourself. Use Guide when available for another look.
3 · ConnectJoin groups into longer passages. A rewind brings earlier digits back for practice.

The opening 3141 advances after one guided entry. Later practice checks it without help. New groups need repeated unaided recall and connections to earlier groups; one successful answer does not always introduce new digits. Misses and help bring difficult joins back more often. A slow answer alone is not treated as guessing.

Enter and correct

ActionGesture
Enter a digitTap, or drag onto it and pause until the small ring fills.
Correct a practice slipLet the wrong digit fade, then try that position again. Accepted digits stay in place.
Undo the latest digitHold it in the four-digit entry row before the fourth digit submits. VoiceOver offers Undo last digit.
ContinueBegin when the next empty entry and dial are ready, even while the previous shape settles into the trail.

Undo removes only the latest digit and its stroke. It preserves earlier mistakes, help, and spent test attempts. Completed groups and Watch cannot be edited. Some trail answers stay hidden during recall.

Read Learning trends

Open Your memory → Learning trends and select a section of up to twenty digits.

VisualRead it this way
Recall & guidanceBlue circles show unaided recall; amber squares show help received. Touch the chart to inspect a saved snapshot and its attempt count.
Group by groupEach row is four digits, with current unaided and help percentages. Stronger color means higher; — means no measured value.
Where slips happenColumns are the 1st–4th positions in each group. Counts and stronger color show where more wrong taps were saved.

Use the group map to compare recall and guidance, then the slip map to locate tricky positions. The chart uses saved snapshots, not daily averages. Its latest snapshot can precede the current group values. These are records of practice, not forecasts of future recall.

For definitions, open What the numbers mean here or in Settings → Help. See the metric reference.

Explore at your pace

ControlWhat it offers
WatchStart from your current group or π; choose 1–8 repeats and Slow, Flow, or Quick speed. Stop to resume your saved input. Watching gives no unaided recall credit.
Sequence → Explore πBrowse 4, 8, or 16 digits with the slider or minus/plus. Tap a group to highlight its shape.
Practice from hereStart continuous practice at the selected place. The named return control restores your previous region and unfinished input. Skipped digits are not marked learned.
Your memory → View your spiralChoose My journey to see practiced groups, or Explore π to include every available group without changing your progress. Play the Memory Spiral, or pause to pan and pinch to zoom. Fit the whole spiral or focus the current movement.

Your Watch speed also sets how quickly completed shapes travel from the dial into the sequence during Guide, Memory practice, and memory tests. The next entry opens after the shape lands.

Viewing answers counts as guidance. After a practice mistake, finish correcting the group before requesting another demonstration.

Test your memory

Choose Test my memory in Your memory or Settings. You can start before completing a lesson and continue beyond your practice place.

  • Memory only. No Guide, Watch, hints, skipping, or timer. The fourth digit checks the whole group.
  • Four attempts per group. A miss retries the same group automatically. Success advances and refills the four marks around the orb. Four misses end the test.
  • Finish whenever you like. Finish is at the top left; Settings is at the top right. Explore and Your memory are unavailable during the test.
ResultMeaning
Uninterrupted recallWhole groups of four before the first mistake. A correct retry does not restart or extend this score.
Digits checkedEach submitted group once, including misses. Retries add no extra digits.
Personal bestCompared with saved tests from the same starting place. Anchor tests have a separate best.

The four attempts let you continue after a mistake; they do not extend your uninterrupted score. For example, 32 digits of uninterrupted recall means the first eight groups were correct before a miss in digits 33–36. If 44 digits were checked, eleven different groups were submitted, including any incorrect groups. Retries do not add extra digits.

An optional anchor note in Your memory enables a test from that section. Tests preserve lessons and review dates. Return to practice restores your saved place, region, and input. An unfinished test also survives closing the app. Older results may retain earlier scoring rules.

Settings, accessibility, and saving

Settings → Extra guidance controls glyph hints and the next-digit highlight. Tests always hide hints. Settings also offers System/Light/Dark appearance and haptics.

With larger text, find Explore, Watch, and playback speed in Practice options. VoiceOver labels the controls and waits for spoken digits during Watch. The app follows text-size, contrast, transparency, and motion preferences. Replay the introduction is in Settings → Help; the Continue Practice shortcut opens saved practice.

Practice, input, reviews, tests, anchors, and settings save locally. There is no automatic cloud sync, progress import/export, or Home Screen widget. The app keeps a previous valid save for recovery and tells you if it restores that copy. Follow any recovery notice; a save from a newer version requires an app update.

Erase all progress and settings removes local records and preferences after confirmation. It cannot be undone. Manage device backups and files exported by older versions separately.

For help, choose Settings → Support and include the app version, iPhone or iPad model, OS version, and what happened. Practice history is not automatically sent.
